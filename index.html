Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Gauge 10 and Gauge 12 aluminum? ▾

In sheet metal measurement, a lower gauge number means thicker metal. Gauge 10 is 3.2mm thick (commercial heavy-duty, ideal for Dum Biryani degs & halwai kadais), while Gauge 12 is 2.6mm thick (ideal for boiling pots, milk cans, and steamers).

Do you deliver bulk orders outside the state / across India? ▾

Yes, we dispatch wholesale consignments daily via trusted transport logistics (VRL, Navata, ARC, TCI, etc.) to all states across India. Heavy degs are packed with reinforced corner pads to prevent denting.

Can we get custom name stamping or embossing on utensils? ▾

Yes! For caterers, hotels, and bulk buyers ordering 10+ units, we offer custom metal name stamping (e.g. your catering company name or hotel brand) on the collar or lid.

Is your aluminum 100% food safe and non-toxic? ▾

Strictly yes. We only use 100% pure virgin food-grade aluminum ingots (IS:21 standard). We never use scrap melted alloys that contain lead, zinc, or impure traces.
